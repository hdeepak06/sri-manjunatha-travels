Suggested Itinerary

Suggested Hampi Day Trip

A curated timeline crafted by local drivers to help you experience the best of Hampi comfortably. Timings are flexible and customized around your train schedule or preferences.

01
08:00 AM • Morning Pickup

Start Journey from Hosapete

Prompt pickup from your hotel or Hosapete Junction. Enjoy a smooth 20-minute drive to Hampi in an air-conditioned vehicle as the morning sun lights up the granite landscape.

02
09:00 AM

Virupaksha Temple & Hampi Bazaar

Visit the active 7th-century sacred shrine on the Tungabhadra riverbanks, receive temple blessings, and stroll down the ancient colonnaded market promenade.

03
11:00 AM

Vijaya Vittala Temple & Stone Chariot

Marvel at the iconic Stone Chariot (featured on India's ₹50 note), 56 musical granite pillars, and the grand carved mandapa halls of the Vijayanagara Empire.

04
01:00 PM

Authentic Karnataka Lunch Break

Relax in an air-conditioned local restaurant. Relish authentic Karnataka thali meals, South Indian specialties, and refreshing tender coconut water.

05
02:30 PM

Lotus Mahal & Royal Elephant Stables

Explore the shaded Zenana Enclosure, the Indo-Islamic Lotus Mahal summer pavilion, and the monumental 11 domed chambers of the royal Elephant Stables.

06
04:30 PM

Sunset at Matanga or Hemakuta Hill

Witness the world-famous golden hour as the setting sun bathes Hampi's red granite boulders, ancient shrines, and the Tungabhadra river in glowing amber light.

07
06:30 PM

Smooth Return Journey

Relax in air-conditioned comfort as we drive you safely back to your Hosapete hotel or drop you at Hosapete Junction in good time for your night train.
